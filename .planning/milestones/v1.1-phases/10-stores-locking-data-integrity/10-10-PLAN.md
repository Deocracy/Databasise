<objective>
Expansion plan (STOR-07). `delete_document` builds its stores from the resolved wiring's `store_namespaces`. It commits every store through prepare-all/commit-all when the delete succeeds, and drops pending ops when the delete is refused or reports `fail` (D-08, Q4). `FaissVectorStore.delete_by_ids` buffers until the flush, so dropping pending ops reverts a vector delete like the other stores.

Purpose: ROADMAP criterion 3 ("a failed ... delete commits nothing ..., vector deletes are buffered and reverted like the other stores, and delete uses the wiring's declared namespaces").
Output: the buffered vector delete, the reworked `delete_document`, one edited existing test, and `tests/stores/test_delete_commit_contract.py`.
</objective>
