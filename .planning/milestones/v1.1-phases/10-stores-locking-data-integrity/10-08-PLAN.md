<objective>
Expansion plan (STOR-05). Capability scoping is enforced per access mode. An explicit `EFFECT_TO_STORE` table replaces suffix splitting. `reads_*` effects yield read-only allowlist proxies and `writes_*` effects yield the writable handle. Lifecycle methods are engine-only on every handle, and the store-less effects are refused without touching `raw_stores` (D-09). The parity audit and the engine's MACH-11 accounting use the same table.

Purpose: ROADMAP criterion 4 ("a node that declares only read effects cannot mutate any store").
Output: the table and proxy in `parts_core/__init__.py`, the scheduler view, the two mirrored mappings, and `tests/runner/test_capability_scoping_modes.py`.
</objective>
