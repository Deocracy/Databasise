<objective>
Every query arm becomes selectable without a promote: parts declare read-namespace capability tokens (`reads_vector:<namespace>`) on a new `Part.reads_namespaces` field, and the capability matcher unions them with the effect set, so local, global and hybrid are told apart (WIRE-01, D-01). Part registration validates effects against the frozen Effect literal, which makes the four advanced query members permanently refused for this contract version (WIRE-16, D-10).

Purpose: ROADMAP Phase 13 criterion 1 (each of the six arms can be selected by a production §18.4 selector without a promote; the selector docstring states which capability set selects which arm) and criterion 5 (no dead API).
Output: the Part field, four part declarations, the matcher helper, registration checks, two docstrings, two new row-test files.
</objective>
