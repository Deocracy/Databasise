<objective>
Close MACH-01's "no self-declaration stands in for the computation" clause with a refusal rather
than a silent ignore, and land the three self-declaration probes as committed, non-vacuous evidence.

Purpose: `databasise/parts/schema.py` currently accepts and discards unknown wiring-node keys, with
an inline comment deferring full wire-time strictness to Falsifier 2 — this phase. Under the current
setting an author who writes `effective_depth: stage` on a node sees no error and may believe the
value took effect. The computation already governs; this plan makes the attempt visible, which is
the house style (refusals over silent fallbacks) and what MACH-01's clause actually asks for.

Output: a named `self-declared-derivation` refusal, wire-time strictness on `WiringNode`, a probe
suite with paired controls, and a probe section in the committed evidence document.
</objective>
