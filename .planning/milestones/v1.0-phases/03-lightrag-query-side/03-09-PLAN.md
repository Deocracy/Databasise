- **Unresolved probe edge — MODAL-01.** Classified `unclassified`, therefore `unresolved`. Recorded,
  not auto-resolved and not dropped. This plan is where MODAL-01's own criterion-2 obligation is
  discharged in evidence.

  index, the expectation is exact agreement on chunk sets and rankings, so the tolerance should start
  at zero and every difference should be a named deviation. If exact agreement turns out not to hold

  deviation with its cause rather than widening the tolerance to absorb it.
- **The form of the declared-deviation record is Claude's Discretion per CONTEXT.md.** It follows
  `FALSIFIER-2-EVIDENCE.md`'s committed-file shape.
- **`workflow.human_verify_mode` is `end-of-phase`**, so criterion 6's human spot-check is a
  `<human-check>` inside this task's verify rather than a blocking checkpoint task.
