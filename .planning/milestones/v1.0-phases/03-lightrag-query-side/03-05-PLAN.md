<objective>
Expand from the proven `naive` slice into the graph half: the keyword extractor and the four
entity/relation lookup and hydrate-expand positions, plus the one graph-store method they need.

the graph. These five positions are where LightRAG's knowledge-graph retrieval actually lives, and
they are the positions the `local`, `global`, and `hybrid` arms exist to select between. `keywords`
is also the single stochastic node D-12's whole pinning design is built around, so its shape has to
support being run once and replayed.

bug suite, and per-body tests.
