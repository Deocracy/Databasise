<objective>
Give callers the read side of the corpus: poll an ingest job to completion, read health, counts and
a paginated document list — and expose every corpus-side operation over REST as thinly as Phase 4
exposed query.

Purpose: ROADMAP criterion 3 requires upload-poll-delete as one caller-visible round trip, and
criterion 4 requires the read surface to be bounded and paginated by construction rather than by
convention. A full corpus or index dump is on this milestone's explicit out-of-scope list because
it is both an MCP anti-pattern and an exfiltration risk.
Output: four new seam methods, the corpus-side REST routes, and the two optional dependencies the
next plan needs.
</objective>
