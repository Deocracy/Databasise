## The amendment

Two clauses.

**First — MACH-03 moves to its true point of first need: the first gate-adjudicated promotion.**
The A/A floor is the promotion floor for a *gate-adjudicated* record — RIG §AA.1 sets the gate's
floor at the A/A null's p95. Phase 7 builds only the operator-asserted path. Under RIG §PR.3 and
CONTRACT §7 that record carries no verdict, no tier-of-decision, and no evidence pointer; it carries

consume the floor stays unavailable for the answer-level and index-side classes for all of v1 under
MACH-09's default posture, and Phase 7's success criterion 3 requires the machine to refuse
promote-next and promote-now under that posture by name. The first gate-adjudicated promotion is
therefore outside this milestone. That is the point of first need, and it is named here.

`03-UAT.md` test 2 (the owner's answer-substance spot-check for q1/q2) and test 3 (the owner's own

measured and committed (03-13), and only the owner's judgment is missing. Their point of first need
is the parity claim itself — the statement that the decomposed LightRAG query side *is* the original
within tolerance. Phase 7 does not make that claim. An operator-asserted promotion of a LightRAG
wiring is, by RIG §PR.3's own words, a promotion with no verdict behind it, not a parity claim.
