<objective>
A bundle version changes whenever its question text or gold ids change, a version whose sealed split was opened is never handed back as unopened, and `bundle.json` records its realised split counts (TOOL-13, D-14, ROADMAP Phase 15 criterion 4).

Purpose: the content hash covers question ids and gold answers only, dedup compares the stored hash field (so changing the scheme would mint a spurious `bundle@v2` for unchanged input), `open_sealed` logs only on the new version, and the realised 17/9/4 split is recorded nowhere.
Output: the `bundle.py` changes, an `EVAL-BUNDLE-V1.md` note, and the TOOL-13 row nodes.
</objective>
