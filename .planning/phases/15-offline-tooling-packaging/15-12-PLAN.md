<objective>
The v1 parity arm answers from the same retrieval it reports, fails on a v1 failure instead of returning an empty success, stops echoing caller keywords as v1's, and refuses a missing parity env file before it launches anything (TOOL-07, D-08, ROADMAP Phase 15 criterion 3).

Purpose: the driver calls `aquery_data`, turns any non-success status into an empty success, echoes the caller's keywords when v1 reports none, then calls `aquery` a second time, so the answer and the ids come from two retrievals; `v1_arm` reads a missing `.env.parity` as empty (IN-01).
Output: the driver and arm changes and the TOOL-07 row file.
