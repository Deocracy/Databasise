<objective>
Admission is enforced when the registry loads: §8 conditions 7, 10 and 11 run for every opaque part once all parts and wirings are loaded, a record must name its part, the record fields are validated, the shared-scope rule keys on `kind`, and invalid depth, scope and kind values are refused at registration (TOOL-16, D-17, ROADMAP Phase 15 criterion 5).

Output: `admit_registry`, its two call sites, the record and field checks, the granted WIRE-13 edit, and the TOOL-16 row nodes.
