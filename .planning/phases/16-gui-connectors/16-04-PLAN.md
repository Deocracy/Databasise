<objective>
An operator can list aliases and page an alias's history, with active and tombstoned flags, from a read that creates nothing (D-09, D-10, D-13a; ROADMAP Phase 16 criterion 3; CONN-06, CONN-07).

Purpose: the console's rollback and retire pickers and the alias timeline need the ledger's own projections. Without them they fall back to a client-side log that misses peer clients' promotions.
Output: `Ledger.aliases()`, `Databasise.ledger_aliases()`, `Databasise.ledger_history()`, the two routes, `_OPERATOR_STORE_RESPONSES`, and the row file.
Sequential: this plan runs after 16-03 on the main tree, because it shares engine.py and operator_rest.py.
</objective>
