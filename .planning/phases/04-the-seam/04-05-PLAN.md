<objective>
Ship the second transport and prove it is the same seam: an optional REST layer over the very same
`Databasise` object, with server-sent-event streaming, refusal mapping, and a conformance test that
asserts both transports produce the same envelope for the same query.

Purpose: ROADMAP criterion 5 is the only phase criterion that cannot be met from inside the library.
It is also where the promote decision recorded in 04-01 either holds or quietly fails — a REST layer
that grows its own selector or redaction logic falsifies "one seam, two transports" while every
test still passes.

Output: `databasise/seam/rest.py`, the `rest` optional-dependency extra, the dual-transport
conformance test, and this phase's COVERAGE.md.
</objective>
