<objective>
The verb closures guard generations by their rollback lineage, so retiring the target of an active rollback is refused and a rollback never lands on a tombstoned lineage (LEDG-04, D-06). They also open and close the ledger deterministically, with a read-only open for rollback and retire, so refused verbs on a fresh root create no file (LEDG-06, D-08). This plan is the only `engine.py` owner in wave 4.

Purpose: ROADMAP Phase 14 criterion 3 (the ledger never keeps serving a tombstoned generation) and the connection and filesystem half of criterion 4.
Output: lineage guards and `with`/`open_existing` in the three closures, two row test files, the race-test update, and the lineage docstrings.
</objective>
