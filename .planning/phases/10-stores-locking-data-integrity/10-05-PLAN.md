<objective>
Expansion plan (STOR-06, second half). A successful run commits all of its writes or none of them through prepare-all, then commit-all (D-07). The kv store stages its DML in an uncommitted sqlite transaction, the vector store writes its `.tmp` pair, and the graph store stages a `multi_transact`. Commits start only when all three have prepared. Any prepare failure aborts all three and raises `StoreFault`. One engine lock per store root serializes the prepare-to-commit window, and the vector store rebases on the on-disk state, so overlapping writers never lose each other's vectors.

Purpose: ROADMAP criterion 3 ("a successful one commits everything"). 10-10 reuses `_commit_all` for delete.
Output: the three lifecycle methods on `StorageNameSpace` and on each store, the engine `_commit_all` and flush lock, the documented commit window, and the new `tests/stores/test_prepared_commit.py`.
</objective>
