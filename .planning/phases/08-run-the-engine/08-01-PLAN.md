Contents: `LOCK_FILE_NAME = ".engine.lock"`; `class StoreLockedError(RuntimeError)` with attributes `store_root: Path` and `holder_pid: int | None` and message `store root {store_root} is held by databasise process {holder_pid or 'unknown'}; one engine process per store root (G-36)`; `acquire_store_lock(store_root: str | Path) -> int` that does `Path(store_root).mkdir(parents=True, exist_ok=True)`, `os.open(<root>/.engine.lock, os.O_RDWR | os.O_CREAT | os.O_CLOEXEC, 0o644)`, `fcntl.flock(fd, fcntl.LOCK_EX | fcntl.LOCK_NB)`; on `BlockingIOError` it reads the holder PID with `os.pread(fd, 32, 0)`, retrying up to 10 times at 0.1 s while the read is empty (holder is between flock and write), closes its fd and raises `StoreLockedError(Path(store_root), pid_or_None) from None`; on success `os.ftruncate(fd, 0)` then `os.write(fd, str(os.getpid()).encode())` and returns the fd.
