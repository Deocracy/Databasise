# Lane 10 findings: Graph, vector, and relational fusion for retrieval

Screened 25, admitted 24 (22 arXiv papers with PDFs filed, 1 ACL paper with ACL PDF filed, 1 code-only project), 1 abstain. All figures below were read off a fetched page on 2026-09-12 (arXiv abs page, paper PDF/HTML, proceedings/DOI page, repo page, or GitHub API).

## (a) Admitted items

**HippoRAG 2 (arXiv 2502.14802, ICML 2025, https://github.com/OSU-NLP-Group/HippoRAG, ~4k stars, MIT).** Continual-learning memory that deeply integrates passages into a schemaless KG and retrieves with Personalized PageRank plus online LLM recognition memory; beats pure-vector RAG on factual, sense-making, and associative tasks. Take: the PPR-over-extracted-triples retriever is the closest existing analogue of MelodyScribe's graph-filing path, and its passage-node linking is the pattern to copy for section-to-graph links. Careful: extraction and filtering run on Llama-3.3-70B with NV-Embed-v2 retrieval in the paper's recipe — far above the 1–3B budget — and online retrieval still calls an LLM per query.

**HippoRAG 1 (arXiv 2405.14831, NeurIPS 2024, same repo).** The original hippocampal-indexing retriever: single-step PPR retrieval matching iterative IRCoT while 10–30x cheaper and 6–13x faster online. Take: the single-step multi-hop result is the best evidence that MelodyScribe's one-forward-pass filing can suffice without agentic loops. Careful: weaker than dense retrieval on low-integration datasets (HotpotQA) due to a concept-context tradeoff; keep vector fallback.

**GraphRAG (arXiv 2404.16130, preprint, https://github.com/microsoft/graphrag, 35949 stars, MIT, pushed 2026-09-08).** LLM-built entity KG, Leiden community hierarchy, bottom-up community summaries, map-reduce global answering; large comprehensiveness/diversity wins over naive vector RAG on global questions at 26–97% fewer tokens depending on hierarchy level. Take: community summaries are the proven design for "global" queries over a filed corpus — MelodyScribe's Folio layer should steal the hierarchy idea. Careful: the repo banner says it is largely in maintenance mode (no new features/PRs); indexing cost is the highest in the lane.

**LightRAG (arXiv 2410.05779, EMNLP 2025 Findings, https://github.com/HKUDS/LightRAG, ~39.3k stars, MIT).** Dual-level (low/high keyword) graph+vector retrieval with an incremental update algorithm for streaming inserts. Take: the only mainstream system with incremental graph updates as a first-class feature — directly relevant to the background revise pass — and the highest-adoption codebase in the lane. Careful: neighbor-expansion retrieval is exactly what PathRAG later shows to be redundant/noisy; do not copy the retrieval fan-out blindly.

**MiniRAG (arXiv 2501.06713, preprint, https://github.com/HKUDS/MiniRAG, 1951 stars, MIT).** Heterogeneous chunk+entity graph with topology-enhanced retrieval explicitly designed so small LMs match LLM-based RAG at 25% storage, plus the LiHua-World on-device benchmark. Take: this is the single most on-thesis system for MelodyScribe — SLM-first graph RAG with a reusable eval — study its entity-chunk edge design for Score sections. Careful: compared against its own benchmark suite; independent replication is thin.

**KAG (arXiv 2409.13731, preprint, https://github.com/OpenSPG/KAG, 9048 stars, Apache-2.0, pushed 2026-01-28).** Ant Group's professional-domain stack: KG–chunk mutual indexing, schema-constrained construction, logical-form-guided hybrid reasoning, concept-level alignment; beats IRCoT+HippoRAG by double-digit EM/F1 on HotpotQA/2Wiki/MuSiQue. Take: mutual indexing (every graph node points back to its chunk) is the concrete mechanism for MelodyScribe's "link this section's vector back to raw text and graph nodes" directive, and its logical-form solver is the template for SQL-vs-graph routing. Careful: heavyweight (OpenSPG engine, NLU/NLI/NLG-tuned backbones); distil the ideas, not the stack.

**TableRAG-Google (arXiv 2410.04739, NeurIPS 2024, code in google-research monorepo `table_rag/`).** Query expansion plus schema retrieval plus cell retrieval with frequency-aware truncation; million-token ArcadeQA/BirdQA benchmarks; exact-match SOTA with sharply cut prompt lengths. Take: the schema-then-cell two-stage pattern is how MelodyScribe should file and query tabular facts without stuffing whole tables in context. Careful: depends on OpenAI text-embedding-3-large and GPT-3.5/Gemini-class solvers in the eval; small-model transfer untested.

**TableRAG-Huawei (arXiv 2506.10380, EMNLP 2025, https://github.com/yxh-y/TableRAG).** Hybrid loop — decompose query, retrieve text, program-and-execute SQL, compositionally reconcile — over parallel text and schema corpora, with the HeteQA multi-hop benchmark. Take: the reconcile step (cross-examining SQL results against retrieved text, weighting by evidential utility) is the missing piece for MelodyScribe's router when vector and SQL disagree. Careful: up to 5 iterative LLM calls per query; flatten to one pass for the harness.

**Zep/Graphiti (arXiv 2501.13956, preprint, https://github.com/getzep/graphiti, ~30.5k stars, Apache-2.0).** Temporal context graphs where every fact carries a validity window, episodes preserve provenance, hybrid semantic+keyword+graph retrieval; 94.8% DMR and up to +18.5% on LongMemEval at −90% latency vs baselines. Take: validity windows are the only shipped answer to temporal invalidation in this lane — required reading for any filed fact with a date. Careful: paper is vendor-authored (Zep team) and the benchmark wins are self-reported; treat numbers as vendor claims.

**Cognee (no paper, https://github.com/topoteretes/cognee, ~30.4k stars, Apache-2.0).** ECL (extract/cognify/load) pipelines unifying vector+graph+relational memory with ontology grounding, 30+ sources, and a single-Postgres (pgvector) backend option benchmarked ~10% faster than split stacks in their CI. Take: the closest existence proof that graph+vector+relational can live behind one interface — i.e. Databasise's three-store thesis — and the Postgres-unified backend deserves a spike. Careful: no peer-reviewed paper; all performance statements are vendor/CI claims; admitted as code project only.

**G-Retriever (arXiv 2402.07630, NeurIPS 2024, https://github.com/XiaoxinHe/G-Retriever, 551 stars, MIT).** kNN seed retrieval, Prize-Collecting Steiner Tree connected-subgraph construction, frozen-LLM generation with GNN soft prompt; cuts graph-LLM hallucination 54% vs prompt-tuning baseline. Take: PCST is the principled fix for "relevant nodes, disconnected subgraph" and is cheap to implement over Cozo. Careful: static retriever, small repo footprint, scene/common-sense/KG evals only.

**GRAG (arXiv 2405.16506, NAACL 2025 Findings, https://github.com/HuieL/GRAG, 65 stars, MIT).** Linear-time divide-and-conquer ego-graph retrieval with learned soft pruning plus dual hard-prompt (hierarchical text) / soft-prompt (graph embedding) generation; frozen LLM + GRAG beats fine-tuned LLM baselines. Take: the frozen-LLM result directly supports the harness thesis (retrieval quality > backbone tuning), and ego-graph indexing bounds retrieval cost. Careful: tiny adoption; generation side needs a GNN encoder MelodyScribe does not have.

**Think-on-Graph (arXiv 2307.07697, ICLR 2024, https://github.com/IDEA-FinAI/ToG, 659 stars, license none detected).** LLM-as-agent beam search over KGs (LLM⊗KG), training-free, SOTA on 6/9 sets, with explicit knowledge traceability/correctability; small-LLM+ToG competitive with GPT-4 in places. Take: the traceable reasoning-path output is the format MelodyScribe's op-lists should mimic for auditability. Careful: up to 2ND+D+1 LLM calls per query — the opposite of one-pass; use for the revise pass, not the hot path.

**RAPTOR (arXiv 2401.18059, ICLR 2024, https://github.com/parthsarthi03/RAPTOR, 1704 stars, MIT).** Recursive embed-cluster-summarize tree with collapsed-tree retrieval mixing abstraction levels; +20pt absolute on QuALITY with GPT-4. Take: the collapsed-tree result (flexible granularity beats fixed layer ratios) justifies per-section "embed in isolation vs embed the rewrite" directives. Careful: build-time summarization is expensive and tree quality inherits the summarizer's errors.

**SubgraphRAG (arXiv 2410.20724, ICLR 2025, https://github.com/Graph-COM/SubgraphRAG, 185 stars, MIT).** A lightweight MLP with parallel triple scoring and directional structural-distance features beats GNN/LLM/heuristic retrievers at 1–2 orders of magnitude less cost; flexible top-K sizing per LLM capacity. Take: central efficiency datapoint — graph retrieval does not need a big model, only distance-aware features. Careful: Freebase-style KGQA evals; transfer to schemaless filed graphs unproven.

**PathRAG (arXiv 2502.14902, AAAI 2026, https://github.com/BUPT-GAMMA/PathRAG, 377 stars, license none detected).** Flow-based pruning extracts key relational paths, ordered by reliability against lost-in-the-middle; beats GraphRAG/LightRAG at ~60%/57% win rates. Take: the redundancy-not-insufficiency diagnosis plus reliability ordering should govern how many ops/sections MelodyScribe emits per paragraph. Careful: repo has no detected license; treat as research-only until clarified.

**StructRAG (arXiv 2410.08815, ICLR 2025, https://github.com/icip-cas/StructRAG, ~170 stars).** Hybrid router (DPO-trained) selects the optimal structure per task — table, graph, chunk, algorithm, catalogue — then structurizes and decomposes. Take: the router-over-formats is the direct precedent for MelodyScribe's per-section directives (file-to-graph vs SQL-row vs rewrite-and-embed). Careful: structurization demands strong comprehension/generation — likely frontier-class at build time; router distillation to 2B is untested.

**LeanRAG (arXiv 2508.10391, AAAI 2026, https://github.com/RaZzzyz/LeanRAG → KnowledgeXLab/LeanRAG, 259 stars, license none detected).** Semantic aggregation that writes explicit relations between summary clusters (fixing "semantic islands") plus bottom-up LCA path retrieval; −46% redundancy. Take: inter-summary relations are what MelodyScribe's revise pass should generate when linking filed sections. Careful: newest item here; no independent replication; license unclear.

**Dense X Retrieval (arXiv 2312.06648, preprint, data repo https://github.com/chentong0/factoid-wiki).** Proposition-level indexing significantly beats passage-level retrieval and downstream QA at fixed budget. Take: atomic-proposition filing is the empirically backed granularity for the "rewrite as propositions and embed" directive. Careful: no method code was found in the paper — only the FactoidWiki data repo; results are single-corpus (Wikipedia-derived).

**GLiNER (arXiv 2311.08526, NAACL 2024 long, https://github.com/urchade/GLiNER, 3591 stars, Apache-2.0).** Small bidirectional encoder for zero-shot any-type NER, CPU/ONNX-deployable, beating ChatGPT/fine-tuned LLMs zero-shot, with a joint RelEx entity+relation head for single-pass KG construction. Take: the prime candidate for the 1–3B extraction step — runs where an LLM extractor cannot. Careful: encoder-only; relation coverage is narrower than LLM OpenIE and needs benchmarking on our paragraphs.

**REBEL (Findings of EMNLP 2021, https://github.com/Babelscape/rebel, 576 stars, license none detected; no arXiv version — ACL PDF filed).** BART-based seq2seq that linearizes and emits 220+ relation types end-to-end, with released weights and pretraining data. Take: a tiny (BART-large/base scale) triple emitter that could run the graph-filing op on-device; fine-tunes to new domains in a few epochs. Careful: 2021 relation ontology (Wikidata-flavored) will mismatch our schema; expect a mapping layer.

**CRAG (arXiv 2406.04744, preprint, https://github.com/facebookresearch/CRAG, ~300 stars, license Other/unverified).** 4409 QA pairs, 5 domains, 8 question types, mock web (220K pages) + mock KG (2.6M entities) + 38 APIs; scoring penalizes hallucination over abstention; naive RAG only reaches 44%. Take: the mock-KG+web harness is the template for comparing MelodyScribe's three stores on one corpus, and its penalty scheme should be copied. Careful: static 2024 snapshot; temporal slices decay.

**RGB (arXiv 2309.01431, AAAI 2024, https://github.com/chen700564/RGB).** EN/ZH benchmark separating noise robustness, negative rejection, information integration, and counterfactual robustness; shows LLMs over-trust retrieved context even when warned. Take: adopt the four-ability split for MelodyScribe eval, especially negative rejection ("do not embed/file this section" must be testable). Careful: news-based, 600 base questions — small and dated.

**RAGBench (arXiv 2407.11005, preprint, dataset https://huggingface.co/datasets/rungalileo/ragbench).** 100k examples × 5 domains with TRACe metrics (Utilization, Relevance, Adherence, Completeness); a fine-tuned 400M DeBERTa beats few-shot billion-parameter judges. Take: TRACe is the metric set for the revise pass, and the DeBERTa result is more small-beats-large evidence. Careful: labels are LLM-generated (human-correlated, not human); dataset only, no maintained code repo found.

## (b) Refutations of brief claims (each from a fetched primary source)

1. "Graph augmentation is a pure win." Refuted by HippoRAG 2 (2502.14802): structure-augmented RAG methods score considerably *below* standard RAG on basic factual memory — the paper exists to fix that regression. Filing to the graph must be selective, not default.
2. "Bigger retrieved context is better." Refuted by PathRAG (2502.14902): the binding constraint on graph RAG is redundancy, not insufficiency; flat concatenation of neighborhoods degrades generation.
3. "Graph retrieval needs a heavy joint model." Refuted by SubgraphRAG (2410.20724): a lightweight MLP with structural-distance features beats GNN/LLM/heuristic retrievers at a fraction of the cost.
4. "Multi-step agentic retrieval is required for multi-hop." Refuted by HippoRAG 1 (2405.14831): single-step PPR retrieval matches iterative IRCoT at 10–30x lower cost and 6–13x lower latency.
5. "Frontier judges evaluate retrieval best." Refuted by RAGBench (2407.11005): a 400M fine-tuned DeBERTa outperforms billion-parameter few-shot LLM judges across domains.
6. "LLMs weigh retrieved evidence against their own knowledge." Refuted by RGB (2309.01431): models prioritize retrieved text over correct parametric knowledge even after explicit warnings — the revise pass cannot trust generator-side arbitration.
7. "Vector similarity approximates reasoning relevance." Refuted by KAG (2409.13731): the similarity–relevance gap plus insensitivity to numbers/time/rules is measured, motivating logical-form and concept reasoning over pure similarity.
8. "Passages are the natural retrieval unit." Refuted by Dense X Retrieval (2312.06648): proposition-level units significantly outperform passage-level units at fixed budget.
9. "GraphRAG-the-codebase is the foundation to build on." Qualified by https://github.com/microsoft/graphrag (fetched): the project declares itself largely in maintenance mode, accepting bug fixes only.
