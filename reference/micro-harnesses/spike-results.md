Ten spikes built by Muse Spark 1.3 agents under `.planning/spikes/` in two rounds, each reproduced by an owner-side re-run of its `run.sh` before the verdict entered the manifest. Round 2 followed deep research 2 (`deep-research-2/REPORT.md`) and used the shared teacher-generated query set (`.planning/spikes/shared/queries-v1.json`, 116 queries, split by document). Every number below is in the spike's own `logs/`.

## Signal for the build

1. **The one-pass mechanism is real at 2B** (002). Embedding and generation from the same decode, isolated sequences, copied prefixes, grammar ops: all measured, all reproducible.
2. **Untrained self-embedding is not usable** (004, confirming lane 1 of the deep research). The `[EMB]` state is neither the last-token state nor a good embedding without training. The dedicated 0.6B embedder is the durable-index baseline until a contrastive adapter beats it, which needs labelled pairs and is the next training spike.
3. **KV modules are second-order at this scale** (005), exactly as the efficiency design argued from the arithmetic. Keep runtime prefix reuse; do not build a persisted module store for the corpus. Any persisted-state path must budget the tie-flip noise; grammar-constrained op decoding is robust to it.
4. **Grammar buys format, not truth** (002): zero-shot ops are vacuous and one-shot ops fabricate evidence spans that Proof rejects. Accuracy is a training result: 003 measured it across sizes and found scale alone does not buy it.
5. **Spec v0.2 candidate confirmed** (003): the frontier teacher and every student size fail Proof's evidence rule when evidence is a character span; with evidence as a verbatim quote the harness resolves to offsets, the teacher reaches 204 accepted ops and students pass 50 to 84 percent of what they emit. v0.2 should replace §5 of the spec; owner review pending.
7. **Size is a cost variable today, not an accuracy variable** (003, D-MS-05): 1B is unusable for op emission without sampling or training; routing is flat from 2B to 8B pre-fine-tune while tokens and seconds per paragraph grow about a hundredfold. Start at 2B-class, fine-tune on the teacher labels (targets: evidence quote must contain the subject or value; ISO dates), and reopen the size question after training. The grammar needs the token cap as its terminator (a 2B decode looped one op about 66 times).
6. **Runtime rules** now in `CONVENTIONS.md`: never prepend BOS for MiniCPM5; tokenize prefix and section as one string; `get_embeddings_ith` indexes outputs, not positions; reject empty sections; enforce context limits chunker-side; one model per process; every load under the GPU lock.

## Round 2 signal for the build

8. **Training closes most of the embedding gap and breaks generation** (006). A contrastive LoRA on the 2B takes test MRR from 0.316 to 0.748 in 147 seconds of GPU against the 0.6B embedder at 0.801, stable across seeds, and collapses op emission to zero parses with the adapter on. The one-pass thesis now needs a generation-preserving recipe (lower rank, a KL anchor, or a joint LM loss) and the training-free controls the research recipe puts first (whitening, centering, marker-bias subtraction). Distillation to the embedder's vectors is worse than plain contrastive; the linear merge of the 0.6B embedder into its generative base is a clean monotonic curve that reaches 0.82 of the embedder.
9. **Prompts do not move embeddings on this corpus** (007): no instruction, prefix, or marker design reaches the 0.05 bar on either model, and the wrong-domain control is harmless. Documents stay bare, the chat-template `doc_prefix` stays by default, and instruction design is not a lever until a keyword-heavy query stream says otherwise.
10. **The extraction prompt is a lever on the 4B, not on the 2B** (008). One-shot with a held-out teacher example is the recommended instruction: it clears the bar on the 4B while keeping one pass, because examples teach verbatim quoting. Two-step scores higher at the cost of a second decode. LightRAG-style wording collapses. Placement cost is model-dependent, so prefix sharing is measured per model.
11. **Serving arithmetic on one 16 GB card** (009): 16 slots saturate greedy decode at about 2470 tokens per second; grammar filtering costs 9x on the CPU side and is the ingest bottleneck, ahead of the GPU; instruction-first ordering doubles the shared prefix and prefix-once prefill saves 15 to 24 percent. "A thousand agents" is a queue over K slots at about 44 sections per minute ingest and about 800 queries per second retrieval. Validity (a third of op lists pass Proof pre-fine-tune) dominates the economics before throughput does.
12. **Pipeline order is settled where measured** (010): one loaded model process serves embedding and ops, ops are filed streamed, entities merge at write time, documents are chunked whole, one worker per model process. The stated fusion rule failed: flat graph and SQL bonuses lower vector-only recall, so the build ships vector-first ranking with graph and SQL as backoff, and rank-gated fusion is an open experiment.
13. **Next spike**: reconcile embedding and generation in one adapter (generation-preserving recipe, whitening controls first, more training documents), and fine-tune op emission on the teacher labels with the one-shot prompt; then re-run the size sweep. The blueprints are in `.claude/skills/spike-findings-melodyscribe/`.
