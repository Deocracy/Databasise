**Claim tagging.** Every normative clause in an `## §A` write-up or an `## §R` row carries the project-wide four-value claim tag plus a trace tag, exactly as `CONTRACT.md`'s Conventions require. [docs-verified] [CONTRACT §Conventions]

**The closed verdict vocabulary and the no-numeric-scores rule (D-03).** Every `## §S` row's Verdict cell holds exactly one of four tokens — `holds`, `holds-with-named-risk`, `does-not-hold`, `unmeasurable-on-paper` — and no other token is permitted in that cell. `holds` — the attack found no defect the evidence does not already answer. `holds-with-named-risk` — the goal or dimension is satisfied but carries a stated, unrepaired residual risk. `does-not-hold` — the attack found a defect the documents do not answer and this pass could not repair. `unmeasurable-on-paper` — the dimension is empirical (test dimensions 13-15) and only local measurement on the built comparison rig, per `RIG.md ## §CM`/`## §EV`, can resolve it; a pointer to that section stands in place of a verdict this phase cannot produce on paper. No row carries a 1-5 or other numeric score: a number would average incomparable properties and imply a precision the paper evidence lacks, the same reasoning `RIG.md ## §AA`'s no-numeric-scores framing already applies to gate verdicts. [docs-verified] [CONTEXT D-03] [RIG §AA]

### F-01: Ledger schema has no field distinguishing a human-authored change from a machine-proposed mutation

**Subject:** `S-G7` (Version-controlled — every part, as the improvement mechanism).

**Attack:** Blind lane given `ARCHITECTURE-RUBRIC.md` goal 7 and the five governing documents plus `SELECTION.md`.
